Materials Design

GuardBuoy

Materials & Competitor Analysis for an Autonomous Water-Rescue Device

Code
DRC-01
Year
2024
Domain
Marine systems, Materials, Product analysis
Type
Team project
Overview

The problem

Our team needed to justify GuardBuoy — a solar-powered autonomous water-rescue device using cameras, AI detection, and thrusters — as a superior investment compared to existing market options. This required demonstrating both competitive advantage and selecting materials that would ensure long-term structural reliability in harsh marine environments.

Approach

The project was divided into two interconnected analyses: a competitive landscape review of existing rescue devices, and a materials selection process focused on durability in salt water and UV exposure over a multi-year service life.

Competitor analysis

We reviewed existing autonomous and semi-autonomous water rescue devices on the market, evaluating them across key dimensions:

  • Response time from deployment to victim contact
  • Payload capacity and victim support
  • Autonomy level (remote-operated vs. fully autonomous)
  • Power source and operational range
  • Cost and accessibility for emergency services

GuardBuoy's AI-based detection system and solar charging differentiator a clear competitive gap in fully-autonomous, low-operating-cost rescue solutions.

Materials selection

Structural reliability in marine environments demands careful materials selection. The key requirements were corrosion resistance, UV stability, impact toughness, and buoyancy contribution. The analysis compared:

  • Marine-grade aluminium alloys (6061-T6, 5052) for structural frame
  • High-density polyethylene (HDPE) for outer hull panels
  • Fibreglass-reinforced polymer (FRP) as a composite alternative
  • UV-stabilised ABS for camera and electronics housing

The final recommendation balanced cost, manufacturability, and service-life performance — with marine-grade aluminium for the chassis and HDPE for the hull, providing a strong corrosion and impact resistance profile.

Outcome

The analysis successfully demonstrated GuardBuoy's technical and commercial viability against existing alternatives, and produced a materials specification with clear justification for long-term marine deployment.
